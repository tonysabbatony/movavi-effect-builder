--- Historical v0.5 notes ---

Implementation: bloom-rework.js. No proprietary Deep Glow code/algorithm used.
Input: sRGB 8-bit Canvas, alpha preserved. Decode piecewise sRGB to linear.
Prefilter BEFORE blur at half resolution: 4 samples at +/- .5 input texel,
brightness=max(linear RGB); threshold also decoded from sRGB; k=max(thresholdLinear*knee,1e-5).
soft=clamp(brightness-thresholdLinear+k,0,2*k)^2/(4*k).
highlight=linearRGB*alpha*max(brightness-thresholdLinear,soft)/max(brightness,1e-5).
Five pyramid levels: ceil(input / 2^(i+1)). Each is prefilter/downsample followed by
separable Gaussian, 5 bilinear fetches per direction; weights .227027027, .316216216,
.070270270 at offsets 0,+/-1.384615385,+/-3.230769231. Gaussian offset multiplier
clamp(radius/50,.25,3). Downsample uses 4 taps +/- .5 previous texel. Clamp-to-edge.
Mix levels using weights linearly interpolated by scatter from [.65,.2,.1,.04,.01]
to [.08,.12,.2,.27,.33]; both sets sum to 1. Composite:
screen=baseLinear+(1-baseLinear)*(1-exp(-glow*intensity*3));
result=encodeSRGB(mix(baseLinear,screen,opacity)); alpha=source alpha.
Intensity=0 or opacity=0 is a bit-exact copy bypass in browser.
Textures allocated on dimensions changes, reused per frame; RGBA16F if renderable,
RGBA8 fallback. 16 draw calls: 5*(prefilter/downsample + H + V) + composite.
